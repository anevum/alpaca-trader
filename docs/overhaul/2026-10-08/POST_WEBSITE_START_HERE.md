## Stop conditions

Stop the migration and use the architecture rollback if any of these appear:

- live order decisions change without an explicitly promoted strategy change;
- reconciliation becomes incomplete;
- protective exits become less reliable;
- scheduler ownership becomes ambiguous or duplicated;
- canonical evidence is lost or cannot be reconstructed;
- NOSTRA uses future information;
- any research/replay/model process gains broker-write authority;
- public/private projections become less truthful;
- resource savings require weakening risk or safety controls.

## Do not do first

- deleting Railway variables;
- deleting the shadow before harvesting its evidence;
- deleting subsystem source/history;
- rewriting the strategy;
- changing options/short/leverage authority;
- merging this package directly to production;
- redesigning the newly completed website again.

The first implementation objective is a behavior-preserving architecture simplification.
