# Strategy 004 — Entry Structure Research

Status: **RESEARCH ONLY — NOT LIVE**

Dataset: September 25, 2026 live session, 36 closed long trades.

## Baseline

- 36 trades: 10 winners / 26 losers
- Closed-trade P&L: about -$0.70
- Average winner: about +$0.053
- Average loser: about -$0.048
- The existing 0–100 opportunity score did not separate outcomes: losers had a slightly higher mean score than winners.

This sample is one trading day and is not sufficient for promotion. The filters below are deliberately isolated from live execution until out-of-sample and shadow validation.

## What separated the two full target hits

SNXX and the first SMCI trade were qualitatively different from the rest of the winner set. Averaged together they had:

- quality score: 90.245
- short-horizon momentum: 0.537%
- session-VWAP edge: 1.076%
- relative-volume ratio: 2.433
- latest completed-bar return: 0.833%
- fast/slow moving-average gap: 0.493%
- bar age: 5.09 seconds

They were not merely high-scoring signals. They combined unusually strong participation, a large fresh impulse, and substantial separation from VWAP.

## Other winners

The other eight winners were much quieter:

- mean momentum: 0.247%
- mean VWAP edge: 0.688%
- mean latest-bar return: 0.062%
- mean quality score: 83.308

This suggests a second valid structure: controlled continuation. The position is already established above VWAP, but the bot is not buying a medium-sized late bar after the move has already accelerated.

## Strategy 004 candidate lanes

### Impulse lane

Requires all of:

- quality >= 88
- VWAP edge >= 0.75%
- relative volume >= 1.5x
- latest completed-bar return >= 0.50%
- at least 2 independent confirmations
- candidate bar age <= 15 seconds

### Continuation lane

Requires all of:

- VWAP edge >= 0.65%
- momentum between 0.12% and 0.45%
- latest completed-bar return between 0% and 0.15%
- at least 2 independent confirmations
- candidate bar age <= 30 seconds
- relative volume >= 0.30x, unless the candidate bar is <= 10 seconds old

Everything else is rejected by the Strategy 004 research gate.

## MFE / MAE finding

For the later trades where Exit Engine v2 telemetry exists, winners generally developed positive excursion before meaningful adverse excursion. Examples:

- NKE winner: MFE +0.340%, MAE 0.000%
- SOXL winner: MFE +0.268%, MAE -0.093%
- MSFT winner: MFE +0.163%, MAE -0.012%

Many thesis-failure losers showed the opposite structure:

- SMH: MFE +0.064%, MAE -0.246%
- SOXX: MFE +0.040%, MAE -0.272%
- LRCX: MFE 0.000%, MAE -0.275%
- TQQQ: MFE 0.000%, MAE -0.160%

The canonical MFE/MAE persistence gap is now closed for future telemetry: the bot emits minute-bucketed `position_metrics` events and the database projects them into the position row. Existing September 25 Exit Engine telemetry was backfilled, populating MFE and MAE for 17 of the 36 positions. The earlier 19 positions predate sufficient Exit Engine v2 excursion telemetry and remain intentionally null rather than inferred from incomplete data.
